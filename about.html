❄️ A Tribute to the Fallen Flash Games

For over two decades, Flash games ruled the internet. They lived on school computers, browser tabs, and countless childhood afternoons. These games were small, creative, weird, fun, and free — built by passionate developers and played by millions.

When Flash was officially discontinued in 2020, an entire universe of memories, stories, and creativity nearly disappeared. Thousands of games — action, adventure, puzzle, racing, and everything in-between — were suddenly frozen in time.

ICEGames was created as a tribute to those lost worlds. A place where old Flash games can live again using modern emulation, keeping their spirit alive so future generations can experience the magic we grew up with.

Our goal is simple:
Keep Flash games alive forever.
Whether you’re here to relive memories or discover classics for the first time — welcome to the GameStation built on nostalgia, ice, and limitless creativity.

❄️ Long live Flash games. ❄️ Long live the creators who made them. ❄️ And long live the players who keep them alive.

🐸 Pepe the Frog — Internet Legend

Pepe has been part of internet culture for years — appearing in gaming chats, forums, Flash game communities, and late-night browsers everywhere.

From *Feels Good Man* to *Sad Pepe*, he represents the emotions we all felt growing up with classic Flash games.
