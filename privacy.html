📋 Introduction

At ICEGames ("we", "us", "our"), we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our digital preservation service.

Preservation First: As a digital preservation project, our primary goal is to preserve Flash games, not to collect user data. We minimize data collection whenever possible.

By using ICEGames, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

📊 Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to provide and improve our service:

Data Type Purpose Storage
Game Play Data Analytics for preservation research Anonymized, 1 year
Account Information Optional account features Until account deletion
Technical Data Service optimization 90 days
Game Preferences Personalized recommendations Local storage only
Important: We do not require personal information to play games. Most data collection is optional or anonymized.

🎯 How We Use Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • Preservation Research: Understanding which games are most played to prioritize preservation efforts
  • Service Improvement: Optimizing game loading and emulation performance
  • Account Features: Enabling game progress saving and favorites
  • Legal Compliance: Meeting our legal obligations
  • Communication: Responding to user inquiries (if provided)

We never sell your personal information to third parties.

🍪 Cookies and Tracking

We use minimal cookies for essential functionality:

Cookie Preferences

Essential Cookies

Required for basic functionality. Cannot be disabled.

Analytics Cookies

Helps us improve preservation efforts. Optional.

Preference Cookies

Remembers your game settings. Optional.

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings or our cookie consent banner.

🤝 Data Sharing

We share data only in these specific circumstances:

  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights
  • Service Providers: With trusted partners who assist our operations (under strict confidentiality)
  • Research Partners: Anonymized data for academic preservation research
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger or acquisition
No Commercial Sharing: We do not share personal data with advertisers or marketers.

🔒 Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption of sensitive data in transit
  • Regular security assessments
  • Limited access to personal information
  • Secure server infrastructure
  • Regular software updates

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

👶 Children's Privacy

COPPA Compliance: ICEGames is committed to protecting children's privacy online.

Our service is designed for users of all ages, but we take special care with younger users:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
  • Accounts for users under 13 require parental consent
  • We do not display targeted advertising to children
  • Parents can request deletion of their child's information

If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

  • Standard Contractual Clauses
  • Adequacy decisions
  • Other lawful transfer mechanisms
